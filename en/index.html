Contribute

Thirty seconds is enough.

The index is only as good as its reports. There are three ways to take part, from the simplest to the most involved.

Report a print
Printed a part for your appliance? Say whether it works, works with adjustments, or not. A comment is optional.
One click
Suggest a compatible part
Know a publication that fits an appliance? Share its address and the appliance's model number.
One link
Publish an original design
Modelled a part? Publish it directly on the index, with its metadata and the licence of your choice.
One file

No result for your appliance? The search is recorded, and you can post a public request. Requests show designers which parts are missing.

Running a Repair Café or a fablab, or publishing spare parts yourself? Get in touch.
