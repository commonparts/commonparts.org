The printable part for your appliance.

Common Parts Access indexes 3D-printable spare parts published on Printables, Thingiverse, MakerWorld and elsewhere, and organises them by appliance. Search by brand or model number; the index sends you to the original publication, with reports from people who printed it.

Find a part Contribute

Why

The parts exist. They are hard to find.

Thousands of printable spare parts are already published: wheels, clips, knobs, hinges, shelf brackets, battery covers. But the platforms that host them are organised by printed object, while you are looking for a part for one specific appliance.

Compatibility information is scattered across titles and comments, over several platforms, and rarely verified. The result: a whole appliance ends up in the bin for want of a few cents of plastic.

Common Parts Access structures what is missing: the compatibility link. This part fits this appliance, and people have confirmed it.

How it works

An index, not a host.

The index is built around the appliance, not the file. Each appliance has its own page, gathering every known printable part, whichever platform it is published on.

01 — Search

By model number

Brand, product name or manufacturer reference, as printed on the appliance's rating plate. Spelling variants and country suffixes are taken into account.

02 — Check

Through reports

Each compatibility carries a level of evidence, based on print reports: works, works with adjustments, does not work.

03 — Print

At the source

The link leads to the original publication. The file stays with its author, under the licence they chose, and the visit goes to them.

Principles

What you can rely on.

  • Link, don't host The index does not republish designers' files. It only hosts a file when its author publishes it here directly, or to keep a backup copy when the source disappears and the licence allows it.
  • Neutral across platforms Printables, Thingiverse, MakerWorld or a personal website: every source is indexed the same way. No platform is favoured.
  • Open data Index data (appliances, compatibilities, print reports) is published under CC BY-SA 4.0, like the Open Repair Data Standard. It belongs to the people who produce it and cannot be captured.
  • No ads, no tracking No targeted advertising, no trackers, no premium accounts. Contributing will never cost anything.

Common Parts Access is not a competing hosting platform, a marketplace, or a printing service.

Scope

Starting with home appliances.

An index that skims every category helps no one. An index that covers one category almost entirely helps everyone looking for a part in it. So the index starts with a single family of appliances, in depth: dishwashers, vacuum cleaners, fridges, ovens and small kitchen appliances.

Their parts are geometrically simple, print well, and their absence often condemns an entire appliance. They are also what Repair Café volunteers see every month.

First up: vacuum cleaners. Categories follow the Open Repair Data Standard, used by community repair networks; the index adds the missing layer, the exact appliance model and its compatible parts.

Contribute

Thirty seconds is enough.

The index is only as good as its reports. There are three ways to take part, from the simplest to the most involved.

Report a print
Printed a part for your appliance? Say whether it works, works with adjustments, or not. A comment is optional.
One click
Suggest a compatible part
Know a publication that fits an appliance? Share its address and the appliance's model number.
One link
Publish an original design
Modelled a part? Publish it directly on the index, with its metadata and the licence of your choice.
One file

No result for your appliance? The search is recorded, and you can post a public request. Requests show designers which parts are missing.

Running a Repair Café or a fablab, or publishing spare parts yourself? Get in touch.

Progress

Where the project stands.

The project moves in stages. It moves to the next one when usage justifies it, not on a date set in advance.

1 — Prove it is useful
A few hundred appliance parts linked and documented, one page per appliance, search by model number, one-click print reports.
Complete when visitors find their part through search engines and leave reports unprompted.
In progress
2 — Open up contributions
Part suggestions by link, public requests, profiles, light moderation. Partnerships with Repair Cafés and designers.
Complete when contributions arrive regularly without being solicited.
Upcoming
3 — Become the reference
Open data export or read-only API, a second family of appliances.
Upcoming
